Remarks

Reports are coalesced to avoid flooding the Cloud Files filter while a provider is producing data. The first report and the terminal report are always forwarded when they are monotonic. A native progress failure is intentionally ignored because progress is diagnostic and must not replace the request's primary transfer result.
